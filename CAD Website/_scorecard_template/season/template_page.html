Market value cut vs. what reached the tax bill

A market value reduction only lowers the client's taxes if it also lowers the assessed value. Below is my read of the three values in the file. Please confirm or correct it.

Market valueThe CAD's estimate of what the property would sell for on Jan 1. This is what we argue and what our 1% fee is based on.
Appraised valueMarket value after special valuations (ag or timber productivity). For a residential book like ours it's almost identical to market value.
Assessed valueAppraised value after the 10% homestead cap or the 20% circuit-breaker cap. Taxes are calculated from this value, after exemptions. If a home is already capped below market, a market cut may not change it.

      Method & open questions

      How this was built

      • Source. The developers' Denton file (denton_protests_2023-2026.csv (Oct 13): one row per protest, 560,458 rows for 2023–2026, with filer, agent, outcome type, notice and final values, roll assessed value, hearing dates, and (2025–26) ARB panel and appraiser. It replaces the values-only view.) from the Oct 6, 2026 drop. Saved in PTP Data\Denton.
      • Our properties. Any protest filed under 245 that season: 2,687 properties with a result in 2026.
      • 2023 agent IDs are mostly blank. In 2023 only 28,970 of 83,842 agent-filed protests carry an agent ID, but every one has the agent name, so firms are grouped by name. Our 2023 protests (1,476) have the name only.
      • Look-alike names. "Ballard Property Tax Protest" (1774250), "Smart Value Property Tax Protest" and "Camel Texas Property Tax Protest" are other firms.
      • Open protests. 4,363 protests in 2026 are still open (3,302 of them Ownwell's), none ours. They're left out of results.
      • Dates. Actual hearing dates are filled for ARB hearings only, so completion timing covers ARB results.
      • Dedupe. One result per account: the closed protest with the lowest final market value; ties go ARB change > informal change > any change > ARB no change > informal no change > no-show > other. Accounts with any open protest are left out.
      • Starting value. The notice market value. Cut = notice minus final market value; win = any cut, or the "Count as a win" threshold.
      • Buckets. Informal = informal settlements and TopLine sign-offs; Formal ARB = ARB hearing outcomes; Other = no-show, dismissed, withdrawn and other closures. TopLine settlements (TOPLINED) are counted as informal, the same as Collin.
      • Firm groups. Codes combined by name: every Ownwell code (Inc, CRE, Residential, Commercial), every Ryan code (not Ryan Law), O'Connor & Associates, Texas ProTax (all individual agents), Kingswood, Cumming, Gill Denson, Ray Tax Group. Firms outside the top 16 roll up into "Other agents".
      • Residential. State code starting with A. Like-for-like matches on Denton CAD neighborhood code: residential $200K–$2M, peer needs 3+ properties in the neighborhood.
      • Fees. 1% of the market cut, or 0.5% when the capped (assessed) value sits below market value after the cut (the cap held). Uses the roll's assessed value; there's no starting assessed value. Standard terms and full collection assumed.
      • Not available (hidden): market cut vs. tax bill and tax savings, market context; appraiser and ARB panel charts (Collin-only); school districts.
      • Questions and data requests for this county are in the overall data-request report.
